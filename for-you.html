NexOS For You

A planned subscription experience designed to bring NexOS to more compatible devices, including selected Chromebooks, ARM computers, and Microsoft Surface devices.

Explore For You

NexOS on your devices

NexOS For You is planned as a subscription-based edition for supported hardware. Our goal is to make NexOS accessible across a wider range of computers through validated device-specific builds and support.

Supported device categories

Chromebooks

Planned support for selected Chromebook models, subject to firmware, bootloader, and hardware compatibility.

ARM computers

Planned editions for selected ARM-based computers with compatible processors and device support.

Microsoft Surface

Planned support for selected Surface devices, depending on their exact model and components.

Device support

Future subscription plans may include device compatibility guidance and installation assistance.

Planned subscription benefits

Device-specific builds

Installation packages tailored to validated supported hardware.

Updates

Planned access to supported NexOS updates during an active subscription.

Technical support

Planned assistance with installation, troubleshooting, and compatibility.

Development status: NexOS For You is planned and in development. Subscription prices, supported device lists, installation packages, and service terms have not yet been finalized. No subscription is currently available.
