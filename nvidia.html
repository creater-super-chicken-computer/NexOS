NVIDIA EDITION

NexOS NVIDIA

A planned NexOS edition for selected NVIDIA-powered computers and platforms, with a focus on graphics, accelerated computing, and future hardware compatibility.

Explore NVIDIA

Designed for NVIDIA platforms

NexOS NVIDIA aims to support specifically validated NVIDIA hardware. Different platforms require different drivers, firmware, architectures, and installation methods.

Edition highlights

NVIDIA GPU support

Planned support for selected NVIDIA graphics hardware, subject to driver availability and testing.

Accelerated computing

Future exploration of supported GPU acceleration and computing workloads.

ARM platform research

Separate development work is planned for compatible ARM-based NVIDIA platforms.

Developer environment

Future plans include developer tools and supported Linux-based development workflows.

Future platform targets

NVIDIA RTX systems

Selected desktop and laptop systems with compatible NVIDIA RTX graphics hardware.

NVIDIA DGX Spark

A future ARM64 platform research target based on NVIDIA GB10 Grace Blackwell Superchip technology.

Development status: NexOS NVIDIA is planned and in development. NVIDIA GPU drivers, ARM platform support, firmware, and installation methods must be validated separately. No installation image is currently available.
