Installation Guide

Learn about the planned NexOS installation process, hardware preparation, bootable USB requirements, and supported platform families.

Step 1 — Check your hardware

Before installing NexOS, your device must match a supported hardware edition.

  • Identify your processor and graphics hardware.
  • Check your device architecture.
  • Verify storage, memory, Wi-Fi, and boot compatibility.
  • Check the official compatibility list when available.

Step 2 — Prepare installation media

The planned installation process will use a verified NexOS image and a USB drive.

  • Obtain an official NexOS installation image.
  • Verify its published checksum.
  • Use a compatible USB imaging utility.
  • Prepare a USB drive with sufficient capacity.
