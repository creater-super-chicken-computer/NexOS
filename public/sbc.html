SINGLE-BOARD COMPUTER EDITION

NexOS SBC

A planned lightweight NexOS edition for selected Raspberry Pi, Orange Pi, and other single-board computers.

Explore SBC

Small computers. Big possibilities.

NexOS SBC aims to bring the NexOS experience to supported single-board computers. Each device will require its own hardware compatibility testing, boot configuration, and driver validation.

Planned device families

Raspberry Pi

Planned compatibility research for selected Raspberry Pi boards and supported ARM hardware.

Orange Pi

Planned support for selected Orange Pi boards, depending on processor and driver availability.

ARM development boards

Future exploration of other compatible ARM-based single-board computers.

Lightweight desktop

A planned desktop experience adapted to the capabilities of supported boards.

Planned features

ARM64 support

Planned 64-bit ARM builds for compatible boards.

Storage support

Future testing for supported microSD and other storage configurations.

Connectivity

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Ethernet, and USB support will depend on each validated board.

Developer projects

A potential platform for learning, experiments, and lightweight computing projects.

Development status: NexOS SBC is planned and in development. Board-specific boot support, graphics, networking, storage, and other drivers must be tested before release. No installation image is currently available.
